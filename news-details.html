25.11.2024

Practical day for city partners in Duisburg

On October 14, the Städtepartnerschaften practical day took place in the Mercatorhalle in Duisburg.

During this interesting and varied day it became clear once again how valuable friendships between cities are. The meeting was organised by the Netzwerkstelle Städtepartnerschaften (Auslandsgesellschaft.de eV).

The mayor of Duisburg, Edeltraud Klabuhn, welcomed the guests and Ludger Siemes, head of the department Europe and International Interests of the State Chancellery of North Rhine-Westphalia, expressed his appreciation for the city twinning. In addition, there were several workshops and information stands. The Euregio Rhine-Waal was also present.

Svenja Arntz, coordinator of the Small Projects Fund ERW (KPF), gave an interesting workshop on this Interreg fund. The KPF is specifically designed for cross-border initiatives with a maximum subsidy of €25,000. There is a simplified procedure for smaller activities of short duration that qualify for a lump sum of €750. The KPF supports projects in the field of education, promotion of cooperation between government institutions, health and promotion of cooperation between citizens.
