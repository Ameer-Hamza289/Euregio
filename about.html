Working area Euregio Rhine-Waal

The working area of ​​the Euregio Rhine-Waal includes on the Dutch side a large part of the province of Gelderland with the areas Arnhem-Nijmegen, West-Veluwe, South-West-Gelderland, parts of North-East Brabant and the northern part of the province of Limburg. On the German side, the working area includes the Kreis Kleve, the Kreis Wesel and the cities of Duisburg and Düsseldorf. The total area covers 8,663 km² and the Euregio Rhine-Waal has 4.2 million inhabitants.
